08 — Disclosure

Responsible Research

Findings are anonymized. The focus here is process and communication, not targets.

Patched

Misconfigured DNS record on a public service

Verified carefully before reporting, wrote a clear reproduction, and the team resolved it. Lesson: a precise, respectful report gets read; a vague one gets ignored.

Acknowledged

Information exposure through verbose responses

Documented impact conservatively and avoided speculation. Lesson: stating only what you can prove builds trust with the receiving team.

Reported

Outdated component with a known weakness

Reported through the proper channel and waiting patiently. Lesson: disclosure is a conversation on the organization's timeline, not mine.

All research is conducted within published scopes and disclosure policies. No systems are tested without authorization.
